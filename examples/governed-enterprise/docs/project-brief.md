# Project brief: governed-enterprise

Compliance profile: governed

## Problem

Support staff cannot see who changed a customer record, which audits require.

## Outcomes

Every change of a customer record shows who made it and when.

## Scope and non-goals

This is a demonstration project; it holds no real product.

## Constraints

Every change goes through the method.

## Method

Method profile: core
Operating mode: team
Integrated methods and tools: none
