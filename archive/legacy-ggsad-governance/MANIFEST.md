# Legacy GG-SAD Governance Archive

This directory contains non-authoritative historical material. Archived files retain their original
content for auditability; they do not govern current product semantics, repository development, or
conformance.

Current authority:

- `docs/method/GG-SAD_normative_method_specification.md` is the leading product authority.
- `AGENTS.md` contains current repository instructions.
- `.planning/` records the active GSD Core 1.10.0 development requirements, roadmap, state, plans,
  and verification.

Both legacy architecture candidates were retired without a replacement. `docs/roadmap.md` was a
historical delivery-status record, while `docs/implementation-roadmap.md` was aspirational. All
eight archived ADRs remained Proposed and were never accepted. The CHG-001 artifacts are historical
change evidence, not current governance or proof of conformance.
