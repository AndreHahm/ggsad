Purpose: roadmap Phase 4 is a hard gate and its third success criterion is that all blocking findings are resolved (fixed) or explicitly dispositioned by the owner before the diff is treated as final (REVIEW-02). Output: the extended check script, the round-2 disposition record, the note's status line and disposition cells, a summary with the phase-close facts for the orchestrator.
