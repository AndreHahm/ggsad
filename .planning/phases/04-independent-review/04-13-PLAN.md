<objective>
Record the repository owner's dispositions of the BLOCKING findings of the independent review of round 3, derive the outcome by rule, and close the plan without ever letting an agent decide for the owner. The review of round 3 has a scope chosen by the owner (the passes named in the note), and this plan states that scope wherever it reports the review. The procedure is that of plans 04-04 and 04-09; read plan 04-09 first (its three tasks are the model of the three tasks here).

Tracer (task 1): the plain question text of the disposition records is made to hold for round 3 (the one function that the printer and the check D3 both use), proven end to end on one good fixture and two seeded defects, and the whole closing chain is run in stage reported on the real round-3 note, printing the counts, the table of findings and the exact questions, and writing nothing else. Task 2 is the owner checkpoint (skipped for the BLOCKING findings when their count is 0). Task 3 records the reply, ties the note to it, runs the closing check and closes the plan.

Purpose: roadmap Phase 4 is a hard gate and its third success criterion is that all blocking findings are resolved (fixed) or explicitly dispositioned by the owner before the diff is treated as final (REVIEW-02). A BLOCKING finding without an owner disposition keeps the phase open; a fix keeps it open until the changed text has been approved and reviewed again; an accept, defer or reject closes that finding with the owner's reason on record, and both the finding and the disposition stay visible.

Output: the extended check script, the round-3 disposition record, the note's status line and disposition cells, a summary with the phase-close facts for the orchestrator.

## Plain language for the owner

The rules of plan 04-10 ("Plain language for the owner", seven rules) apply to the checkpoint of task 2: a few sentences of everyday words with a concrete example before asking; questions of at most two sentences; option labels of at most four words with one short description; no option recommended; an unclear question is explained in the chat and not sent again; recorded questions are the printed ones; the name and the date are typed by the owner.
