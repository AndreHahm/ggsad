<objective>
Record the repository owner's dispositions of the BLOCKING findings of the independent review, derive the outcome by rule, and close the plan without ever letting an agent decide for the owner. Tracer (task 1): the check script gains the stage dispositioned and the skeleton printer, proven end-to-end on one good fixture built outside the repository, and the whole closing chain is run in stage reported on the real note, printing the counts and the exact question set and writing nothing else. Task 2 holds no script growth: the complete seeded fixture list (every failing case, the fix case and the zero-findings case). Task 3 is the owner checkpoint (skipped when no BLOCKING finding exists). Task 4 records the reply, ties the note to it, runs the closing check and closes the plan.

Purpose: roadmap Phase 4 is a hard gate and its third success criterion is that all blocking findings are resolved (fixed) or explicitly dispositioned by the owner before the diff is treated as final (REVIEW-02). A BLOCKING finding without an owner disposition keeps the phase open; a fix keeps it open until the changed text has been approved again and reviewed again; an accept, defer or reject closes that finding with the owner's reason on record, and both the finding and the disposition stay visible.

Output: the extended check script, the disposition record, the note's status line and disposition cells, a summary.

Shorthand as in 04-01-PLAN.md: SPEC, CHK, CHK2, CHK3, BRIEF, NOTE, DREC, ARC, IDN, REQ, WORK (a `mktemp -d` directory outside the repository, removed at the end of the task that made it, never named in a committed file). The flagged assumptions A-1 to A-9 of 04-01-PLAN.md apply; this plan adds none and resolves none. A-3 and A-4 are applied here: the reviewer's severity is the only classification, and the owner may reject a BLOCKING finding with a reason.

What follows each outcome (described here, not pre-written):
- no blocking findings or blocking findings dispositioned: the diff is final as pinned; the orchestrator verifies Phase 4 and ticks REVIEW-01 and REVIEW-02 at phase close; Phase 5 may then start.
- fix pending: the phase stays open. `/gsd-plan-phase 4 --gaps` plans gap-closure work in this order: edit the specification for the finding; record the diff identity again with the new pins (a new text means a new blob, SHA-256 and line count); regenerate the approval packet and obtain a new explicit owner approval of the new exact text; run Devin again on the changed parts under a new consent (the sections that changed plus the cross-cutting pass); record the dispositions again. This record stays as the record of the first round and its pins no longer match the live text.
