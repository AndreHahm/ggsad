Record the diff identity of the final text of the fix round, print an approval packet that shows the owner the nine changes in plain words with the real before-and-after text and the exact change line by line, ask the owner ONE approval question through the orchestrator, and record the reply verbatim in a record that pins the exact new text. Tracer (task 1): the whole chain is run once on the thinnest path: the pins of the final text are read, the identity note is updated in place, the stages approval-pending and approval-recorded and the four printers are built, the packet is printed and committed, a filled fixture of the record passes and seeded defects fail for the right reason. Purpose: roadmap Phase 4 and requirement APPR-01 (the exact text, as it will be merged, approved by the owner) apply again, because the fixes changed the text that was approved. The approval of the first text is void and stays as history. The owner now approves, or requests changes to, the exact result; the five groups marked `my choice` in the register are exactly the places where the owner can prefer another reading.

Output: the identity note with the new pins, the packet, the record (written only after the reply), the extended script, a summary.

What follows each outcome (described, not pre-written):
- Approved: the record pins the text for the independent review of the changed parts (a reviewer that is not the Requestor; a fresh context or subagent of the Requestor is not independent), which plan 04-08 prepares, runs and records; this plan does not start it. Any later edit of the specification voids the approval.
- Changes requested: the phase stays open. A further gap-closure plan (`/gsd-plan-phase 4 --gaps`) edits the specification for the requested changes, the identity is recorded again, the packet is printed again and the owner is asked again; this record stays as the record of this round, and plans 04-08 and 04-09 wait.
