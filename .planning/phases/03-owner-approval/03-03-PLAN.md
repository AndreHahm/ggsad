<objective>
Obtain the repository owner's explicit answers to the fifteen questions of the packet, record them verbatim in a separate record that pins the exact text, point the packet at the record, and run the closing check. Tracer (task 1): the whole closing chain is run in stage pending on the committed packet and the exact question set is printed from the record skeleton, writing nothing. Task 2 is the owner checkpoint. Task 3 records the reply and closes the plan.

Purpose: roadmap Phase 3 is a hard gate. It cannot complete without the owner's explicit recorded approval of the diff exactly as it will be merged (success criterion 3), and it may not be bundled with any other phase's work. A reply of 'changes requested' is also a valid, recorded reply; it keeps the phase open.

Output: the record (written only after the reply), a one-line change of the packet, on approval one sentence of the diff identity note, a summary.

Shorthand as in 03-01-PLAN.md: SPEC, CHK, CHK2, PKT, REC, REG, IDN. Scratch files go in the session scratch directory or `mktemp -d`, never in the repository, never named in a committed file.

Flagged assumption A-1 (APPR-01 manual edge, probe category 'unclassified', status 'unresolved'): the diff the owner approves is the whole of SPEC as identified by content (base blob to result blob); a changed answer to an owner question or an overruled drafting choice is a requested change and yields 'changes requested'. The packet states this to the owner (section 'What you are asked to decide'); the owner can correct it in any answer.

What follows each outcome (described here, not pre-written):
- Approved: the record pins the text for the independent review of Phase 4 (a reviewer that is not the Requestor; a fresh context or subagent of the Requestor is not independent); this plan does not start it. Any later edit of SPEC voids the approval and needs a new packet and record.
- Changes requested: the phase stays open. `/gsd-plan-phase 3 --gaps` plans gap-closure work that edits SPEC for the owner's requested changes; the diff identity note is re-recorded with the new pins; the packet is regenerated and the owner is asked again; this record stays as the record of the first round and its pins no longer match the live text.
</objective>
